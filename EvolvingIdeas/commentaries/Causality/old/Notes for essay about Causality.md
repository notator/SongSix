1. **Notes on Mind and Cosmos**

I've finished making pencil notes in my copy of Nagel's *Mind and Cosmos* and now want to start collecting thoughts *here* while reading it through again.

Before I start, here are the most important things to bear in mind (recorded on the title page in pencil):



* Nagel seems unaware of Donald Hoffman's insight (that I share) that we don't perceive the world as it really is: that we have an interface of perception that provides us with a simplified version of reality that allows us to survive \[see pages 108, 111, 112, 125]
Hoffman's insight provides a much simpler answer that agrees with Nagel's subtitle (that the materialist neo-Darwinian conception of nature is almost certainly false) without having to postulate controversial things like "value realism". In fact Hoffman's insight contains both subjectivism and realism, that seem for Nagel to be mutually exclusive.
* Nagel, I think, uses the word "idealism" in the original Berkleyan sense of "reality is a creation of the mind".
* The Origin of life: see my comment (Aurobindo etc.) on Nagel's Closer to Truth interview (copied to the [draft of my *Time and Meaning*](https://james-ingram-act-two.de/writings/SongSix/EvolvingIdeas/commentaries/TimeAndMeaning/TimeAndMeaning.html)) \[see page 6 note]
* Nagel seems to accept the Big Bang as the beginning of time (maybe tentatively). I prefer Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology, so there is no beginning of time. (See also the Claude chat re Stockhausen/YLEM in the [draft](https://james-ingram-act-two.de/writings/SongSix/EvolvingIdeas/commentaries/TimeAndMeaning/TimeAndMeaning.html)).

   * "*Natural teleology, common in classical philosophy, though controversial today, contends that natural entities also have intrinsic purposes, regardless of human use or opinion.*".
In the summary of my *The Arrow of Time* it currently says: "*The Universe has an intrinsic temporality that is interpreted as an Arrow of Time (a directional flow) by our necessarily limited senses."* So I'm already using some kind of teleology there, even though I didn't realise that I was doing so. This concept needs some more amplification/attention in the new essay in the light of the Wikipedia article. Note that teleology originally has to do with *causation* and *purposes **regardless of human use or opinion.*** Both causation and purposes are on this side of the dashboard (affected by the dashboard hypothesis). Its a consequence of the dashboard that teleology can be taken more seriously -- we have no idea of what is really behind the dashboard.
This is Hoffman's opinion too. See YouTube: [“Science Will Never Be Anywhere Near the Truth”: Donald Hoffman with John Vervaeke](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cWEdAnIULIQ) -- Apropos: I posted the following comment there (21.11.2026)

     * Donald Hoffman is also one of my heroes, but I think he could have given better answers to two of the questions raised at the end:

[45:16](https://youtu.be/cWEdAnIULIQ?t=2713): Iain McGilchrist's question mentioned live music making, skirting the problem that musicians work with perceived time, not the "time" in the (mathematical) spacetime model.
[53:29](https://youtu.be/cWEdAnIULIQ?t=3206): Àlex Gómez Marín asked whether and how it might be possible to falsify the Interface Theory of Perception
In answer to the latter question Hoffman said (at [55:01](https://youtu.be/cWEdAnIULIQ?t=3301)): "If I can't give you what we currently take to be \***our spacetime**\* and its properties as a particular instance of the recursive trace logic, then I'm wrong."
Showing that the theory implies the current standard model of spacetime would show that its at a deeper level, and add it to the standard model. But we know that the standard model is not a complete description of reality, so that doesn't prove anything one way or the other.
The *Interface Theory of Perception* seems to belong with Quantum Theory and the Theory of Evolution, in a meta-scientific category: These are useful ways of thinking that lead to progress, but which cannot be proven or disproven either empirically or with mathematical certainty.

Hoffman said at the beginning ([0:04](https://youtu.be/cWEdAnIULIQ&t=4)) "What we perceive in our spacetime headset could be utterly and completely unlike anything in objective reality."
So he could have answered McGilchrist's question better by emphasizing that, as a physicist, he is talking about the physicists' mathematical model of spacetime, not the space and time musicians (and everyone else) actually perceive. The *Interface* in his own *Theory of Perception* reduces the amount of information that has to be processed by giving us a glimpse of ultimate reality (currently modelled as spacetime) in the form of *separately perceived* space and time! 🙂
