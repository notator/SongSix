  4. Circles as symbols of the world (relates to teleology and Penrose/Turok CCC):
    1. See my Short Introduction to Krystals
    2. Claude says: Several traditions treat the enclosed space of a circle as the world or a model of it.
      The first two or three of the following are the most firmly documented as "inside the circle equals the world." The magical and occult readings are often more interpretive and come from later writers.
      • **Medieval T-O maps.** The world map was a circle, the *orbis terrarum*, with the known continents inside and the encircling ocean as the rim. The "O" is the world, and the "T" inside it is the Mediterranean and the rivers dividing Asia, Europe, and Africa.
      • **Ancient Greek cosmography.** In Homer, the shield of Achilles shows earth, sea, sky, and the stars within a circular frame, with the river Okeanos running around the rim. The disc of the world surrounded by ocean is a very old image.
      • **Mandalas.** In Hindu and Buddhist practice, the mandala's inner space is a model of the cosmos, often with Mount Meru at the center. In Tibetan "mandala offerings," the practitioner mentally offers the entire universe, symbolized by a circular heap of rice or grain on a disc.
      • **Roman city foundation.** Plutarch's account of Romulus founding Rome mentions a circular pit called the *mundus*, which he describes as an image of the cosmos. Scholars debate how much of this reflects actual Roman practice, but the idea of a bounded circle as a world in miniature is there.
      • **Western ceremonial magic.** In grimoires like the *Key of Solomon*, the magician's circle is a protected microcosm. Inside is ordered, sacred space with the practitioner at the center, and outside is the dangerous, chaotic world. Later occult writers often interpret the circle itself as the universe.
      • **Native American traditions.** Many Plains nations, in the way they're described by figures like Black Elk, see the circle as the shape of the world, the sky, the seasons, and life itself. In medicine wheel teachings, the space inside represents all of creation. Practices vary a lot between nations, so I'd be cautious about generalizing.

On 21.11.2026 I posted the following comment to
“Science Will Never Be Anywhere Near the Truth”: Donald Hoffman with John Vervaeke
Donald Hoffman is also one of my heroes, but I think he could have given better answers to two of the questions raised at the end:
45:16: Iain McGilchrist's question mentioned live music making, skirting the problem that musicians work with perceived time, not the "time" in the (mathematical) spacetime model.
53:29: Àlex Gómez Marín asked whether and how it might be possible to falsify the Interface Theory of Perception
In answer to the latter question Hoffman said (at 55:01): "If I can't give you what we currently take to be *our spacetime* and its properties as a particular instance of the recursive trace logic, then I'm wrong."
Showing that the theory implies the current standard model of spacetime would show that its at a deeper level, and add it to the standard model. But we know that the standard model is not a complete description of reality, so that doesn't prove anything one way or the other.
The Interface Theory of Perception seems to belong with Quantum Theory and the Theory of Evolution, in a meta-scientific category: These are useful ways of thinking that lead to progress, but which cannot be proven or disproven either empirically or with mathematical certainty.

Hoffman said at the beginning (0:04) "What we perceive in our spacetime headset could be utterly and completely unlike anything in objective reality."
So he could have answered McGilchrist's question better by emphasizing that, as a physicist, he is talking about the physicists' mathematical model of spacetime, not the space and time musicians (and everyone else) actually perceive. The Interface in his own Theory of Perception reduces the amount of information that has to be processed by giving us a glimpse of ultimate reality (currently modelled as spacetime) in the form of separately perceived space and time!🙂
