Curriculum Vitae
I've always been interested in music, music notation and the philosophy of time.
Born in England in 1948, my philosophical roots are in the English empiricist tradition as it was in the 1950s and 60s. I was reading Russell and Popper in my teens, and continued to read books on the philosophy of time and symbols well into the 1980s. Devoured Douglas Hofstadter's Gödel Escher Bach in early 1981.
My outlook has also been shaped by having moved to Germany in 1977. The Arts are taken far more seriously here than they are in the UK. This obviously has to do with the German Romantic tradition. Romanticism is anti-materialism. Its another way to understand the world. Think Kant, but also Wagner and Nietzsche...
Following the discovery of Donald Hoffman and Bernardo Kastrup online (2016 and 2022 respectively), I realised that I've actually been an Idealist all along.
The score of my beyond the symbolic (1982) contains the following poem in its preface:
that more is
but beyond words
is will be misunderstood
Being self-taught in philosophy, I inevitably have blind-spots, so it was both a shock and a pleasure to discover the British Idealists (Bradley and Collingwood in particular) via Nathan Hawkins' YouTube channel in 2025.
Bradley is only mentioned in passing in Russell's encylopedic History of Western Philosophy. Collingwood is not mentioned at all!
I've always been sceptical of the institutional structures that have grown up around the Arts and Sciences, and became Karlheinz Stockhausen's principal copyist (1974-2000) because I thought academia was not going to be able to solve the 20th century music notation crisis. Indeed, academic institutions were part of the problem, not the solution. They were eating their own children.
This scepticism also lies behind my belief that contemporary philosophy needs input from both professional philosophers (historians of philosophy) and those with direct experience of the world outside.
My own, specialized experience is with music symbols and programming. Started programming in 1982 (Apple II). Since 2000, I've been programming music-related, open source applications in C# and Javascript, and maintaining this website.
Rhizoome
a.k.a.
'Hans'
My native language is German. While I love English for its ability to express concepts simultaneously simply and precisely, I've come to appreciate the very intricate, sometimes complicated precision of German in philosophy.
In philosophy, I progressed from the Greek Classics to Phenomenology, Idealism and then to New Realism. One critique of New Realism is that it is Idealism in disguise, so perhaps I am stuck at Idealism. My background is in the German continental tradition but I continue to have great respect for the ancients (in particular, Aristotle's Nicomachean Ethics).
I've been writing software since 1995. My main languages are C, Lua and Python. Until about 2014 I learnt almost every language that was common at the time. Since around 2020 I have been very disappointed by how little the Free Software community has been able to do about the digital dystopia we are living in. Since then I have been focusing on frugal software in C. Recently I developed a lot of enthusiasm for the C dialect by Ken Thompson and the accompanying C style by Rob Pike. They are perfect for my autistic mind.
After trying social media for a year, I found that I hated it and gave up. Since 2017 I have stopped following global news, I only learn about it once I think it has become history. I'd rather spend my energy and psychological resources on positive local action. I am involved in a few preservation and sustainability projects.
